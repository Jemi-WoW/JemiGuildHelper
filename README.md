# Jemi Guild Helper

A WoW Forever addon that adds its own tab to the Guild & Communities window.

## What's in it

- **Recruitment** - post your recruit message, scan /who for players without a guild, and read every whisper in one place. Players you already contacted stay hidden for 3 days, and recruiters share that list.
- **Crafters** - see what everyone in the guild can craft, and search by item or profession.
- **Raids** - officers plan raids, everyone signs up. Loot rolls follow the raid's reserves.
- **Guild map** - guild members show up on the world map.
- **Dings** - a popup when someone levels up or maxes a profession.

## For Guild Masters

Open Guild Settings and pick **Jemi Guild Helper** in the Guild Control dropdown. There you can:

- choose which ranks can use Recruitment (the top two by default)
- set your Discord invite (officers can change it too)

## Using it

Click the minimap icon, or press `J` and pick the peon tab. `/jgh help` shows the commands.

Blizzard blocks addon invites on Forever, so Invite gives you `/ginvite Name` to paste.
