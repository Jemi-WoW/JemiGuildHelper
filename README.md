# Jemi Guild Helper

Guild recruiting and a guild crafters directory, built right into the Guild & Communities window.

Runs on **WoW Forever**.

## Features

- **Lives in the guild window** — a new tab on the right side of Guild & Communities, no extra window
- **Recruit Message** — write your ad, tick channels, see a live chat preview, post one channel per click
- **Fast Invite** — scan by level range and class, players already in a guild are skipped
- **Quick invites** — Invite hands you `/ginvite Name` ready to paste (Blizzard blocks addon invites on Forever)
- **No double spam** — contacted players hide for 3 days, declines stay visible with a count
- **Whispers** — every whisper in one inbox, reply right there
- **Recruiter sync** — recruiters share who was already contacted, even across layers
- **Crafters** — for every guild member: search who can craft an item, browse by profession or Auction House category, see each member's recipes and whisper them
- **Automatic** — your recipes are read at login and shared with the guild, new ones as soon as you learn them
- **Guild map** — guild members show on the world map with their class icon, level and professions; members inside a dungeon line up above its entrance.
- **Raid sign-ups** — officers plan Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit, Onyxia's Lair or any other raid; everyone signs up as Tank, Healer, Melee or Ranged, or marks Late, Tentative, Bench or Absent, with a reminder 15 minutes before
- **Loot rolls** — the raid leader rolls off each drop from the loot window; everyone gets a roll popup that follows the raid's soft and hard reserves, and /roll from players without the addon counts too
- **Join Discord** — a button in the guild window title bar copies your guild's Discord invite; the Guild Master or an officer sets it in Guild Settings > Guild Control > Jemi Guild Helper (it is saved in Guild Info, and invites already in Guild Info are picked up)
- **Guild dings** — a popup when a guildmate levels up or maxes a profession, with a Grats button for guild chat
- **Rank access** — the Guild Master and the rank below can recruit by default; the Guild Master picks which ranks in Guild Settings > Guild Control > Jemi Guild Helper, and everyone else sees Recruitment locked

## Using it

Click the minimap icon, or open the guild window with `J` and pick the peon tab. It opens where you left off.

`/jgh help` lists the rest.
